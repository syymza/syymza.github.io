MinuteLoom privacy policy

Publisher: Daniele Zanni. Effective date: 13 November 2026.

Your time records

MinuteLoom stores the projects, client labels, rates, currencies, session times and notes you enter in the app's local storage on your device. It uses these records to track time and calculate reports. The app does not require an account and does not automatically synchronize your ledger between devices.

MinuteLoom has no developer-operated server, advertising or analytics integration that receives your ledger.

Exports, imports and device backups

You can choose to export CSV or PDF reports and JSON backups through Apple's file and sharing interfaces. Those files can contain project and client labels, rates, session details and notes. Where the files go depends on the destination or recipient you select, which can include a cloud file provider. A selected recipient or provider handles the copy under its own terms and privacy practices.

MinuteLoom does not add encryption to exported reports or JSON backups. Protect these files and share them only with intended recipients. The app does not control the security or retention of copies after you export or share them.

Your device backup settings may include app data. MinuteLoom does not promise that the ledger is excluded from device backups. Apple and any file provider you choose manage their respective services.

Retention and deletion

The working ledger remains in the app's local storage. The current app lets you edit sessions and archive projects; archiving keeps the project's history and reports. It does not currently provide an in-app deletion workflow. Exported, shared and device-backup copies are separate from the working ledger. Manage those copies in their respective destinations and consult the relevant provider for its deletion controls.

There is no MinuteLoom account or developer-hosted ledger to close or erase. If you choose to remove all local app data using the device's app-deletion controls, first keep any records you need: deletion can remove your only copy. Offloading an app is not the same as deleting its data. Device backups and previously exported or shared files must be managed separately. Recovery of deleted or missing data is not guaranteed.

Support and privacy questions

Contact Daniele Zanni at daniele@duck.com. When you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to your request, investigate the issue and provide related follow-up. Email service providers process messages for delivery and storage. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the request and follow-up, or to meet legal obligations.

Do not send private ledger files unless necessary. Redact client names, notes and other private information where possible. We cannot remotely access the ledger stored on your device. Contact us with questions about personal information you have sent to support, including requests about access or deletion.

This website

These static support pages use GitHub Pages and contain no analytics, advertising, scripts or contact form. GitHub may process technical connection information, such as your IP address, when serving the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if app or support practices change.
