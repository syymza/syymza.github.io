Privacy

Spin Sprout stores your wheels on this device. It has no account, ads, analytics, network service or runtime AI. Your device backup may include app data. Files and results leave the app only when you choose to export or share them.

Delete a wheel to remove it from the app. Exported files and device backups are managed separately by you.

Appearance preferences are saved on your device. Destinations you choose for exports or sharing have their own privacy practices.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including your IP address, to provide and protect the site. This is separate from the app. If you email support, we receive your message and the information you include, and use it to reply. Do not send passwords or private backup contents. Contact the support address below for privacy questions.
