Privacy policy · Last updated 5 November 2026

Done & Dated has no advertising, analytics SDK or tracking. You do not need an app account to keep records locally.

Who provides the app

Done & Dated is provided by Daniele Zanni. For privacy questions, contact daniele@duck.com.

Records on your device

Activities, dates, notes, categories, tracking states, reminder choices, selected photo copies and display preferences are saved in the app’s storage. The current local-first app does not send your records to a developer-operated server or track you across apps or websites.

iCloud and other cloud services

Automatic iCloud sync is not currently available in the app. Apple device backups and exports to Files are separate: whether they are stored in a cloud service depends on your Apple settings and the destination you choose. The app does not ask for your Apple Account password and does not automatically send exported files to us.

Photos and sharing

The system photo picker gives the app only the photos you select. The app stores resized JPEG copies for your entries, rather than requesting access to your whole photo library. Taking a photo requires camera permission. Captures are not added to Photos automatically.

Sharing a photo or entry card opens the system share sheet. The destination you choose receives the image. Saving an image to Photos requires the appropriate permission. Deleting an attachment or its last entry removes its live app copy after saving; your original in Photos is unchanged. Earlier recovery copies may still contain it.

Backups, exports and recovery

Apple device backups may include app data, depending on your device and iCloud settings. Before a restore changes records, the app retains three recent local recovery copies with records and photos. They are not automatically sent anywhere. You can review, export or remove them in Recovery copies.

A manual export includes records and attached photo copies without encryption added by this app. The Files destination you choose may sync through iCloud Drive or another provider under its own terms. Exported files and system backups are managed separately from the app. Keep them somewhere you trust.

Reminders, Shortcuts and widgets

Optional reminders are scheduled locally after notification permission. Activity names may appear on the Lock Screen. Apple manages system permissions and Shortcuts. Widgets receive active activity names, icons, last-recorded times and any usual rhythm interval you set, but not notes or photos. Widget content may appear on the Home or Lock Screen.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in current history until you delete them or remove the app. Archiving keeps history. Recovery copies can retain deleted records and photos; remove those copies separately. Removing the app deletes its local storage. Copies exported elsewhere and Apple backups are controlled separately through Files, your provider or Apple settings. Restoring a backup may bring records back.

When you contact us

If you email daniele@duck.com, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Please send only what is needed and never include passwords or verification codes. Contact the same address for questions about support correspondence or a deletion request.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. We add no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or third-party fonts to this site.

Policy changes

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change. Any future cloud feature must be described here and in the app before it is made available.
