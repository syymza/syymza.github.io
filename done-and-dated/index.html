Done & Dated helps you remember the last time you did something.

Contact support

Start with one activity

Add something you want to remember, such as watering a plant or changing a filter. Tap Log now when you do it. The activity shows how long it has been.

Correct a record

Open an activity and select an entry in its history to change its date, note or photos. Use Undo after a new log if you tapped by mistake.

Keep a separate backup

Open the More menu, then Data and settings, and choose Save a full backup. Exported backups include your attached photo copies. Keep them somewhere you trust. Review the preview before restoring.

Find an earlier local copy

Before a restore changes your records, the app keeps recent recovery copies on this device. You can review, export or remove them in Recovery copies. They are separate from backups you export.

Reminders

Reminder delivery depends on notification permission, Focus and device settings. Open the activity’s options to review its reminder. Core logging works without notification permission.

Something not working?

Email your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. Never send passwords or verification codes. Include private records or photos only if necessary and you choose to share them; redact information that is not needed.
