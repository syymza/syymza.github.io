Note Porch privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Note Porch is provided by Daniele Zanni. For privacy questions, email daniele@duck.com.

This policy describes the Note Porch iPhone and iPad app, its widgets, user-directed file exports, support correspondence and these support pages.

Local notes and settings

Note Porch works offline. It has no user account, app-managed backend, advertising SDK, tracking tools, analytics SDK or app-managed cloud synchronization. The app does not upload your notes to us, and we cannot view or recover them remotely.

The app stores note titles and text, color presets, text size and alignment, daily times, note identifiers and update information on your device. Note data is held in a local shared container so the app and its widget extension can use the same notes. App appearance preferences are stored locally as well.

Notes may contain personal information if you choose to write it. Device protections depend on iOS or iPadOS and your device settings. Note Porch is not a password vault and does not provide its own note-encryption password or app lock.

Widgets and visible information

Widgets display note text on the Home Screen or Lock Screen, where other people may see it without opening Note Porch. Widget configuration also uses note titles to help you select a note. Do not use widgets for passwords or sensitive information.

The app marks widget views as privacy-sensitive for the system, but this is not an authentication or confidentiality guarantee. iOS controls refresh timing, rendered snapshots and configuration interfaces. An older widget display or cached selected-note title may remain after a note is edited or deleted, until the system updates it or you change the selection.

Backups, imports and file providers

When you choose Export Notes, the app creates a JSON file containing your notes, their appearance choices and your daily plan. Export Recovery Files creates a separate file containing raw current, previous and archived data, including older or imported note files when present.

You choose the destination through the system Files interface. A selected location can be on your device or with iCloud Drive or another file provider. A cloud provider can upload and retain that file under its own settings and privacy practices. This is a user-directed export, not automatic synchronization operated by Note Porch.

Exported backups and recovery files contain readable note data and are not separately encrypted or password-protected by Note Porch. Anyone with access to a file may be able to read its contents. Choose storage locations and sharing permissions carefully.

When you import or restore, Note Porch reads the file you select and copies its data for review. It does not keep ongoing access to that external file or synchronize future changes. Import adds missing notes without replacing existing notes or changing the daily plan. Restore replaces current notes and the daily plan after your confirmation.

Previous snapshots and recovery archives

Normal saves keep a previous local snapshot to support recovery. Import and recovery operations also preserve original local files and, when applicable, the incoming file in local recovery archives before changing the active notes. These copies can contain earlier versions of notes, including information you later change or delete.

Recovery archives remain locally until you remove them using the app's separate cleanup confirmation, or the app's local data is removed. After exporting recovery files successfully, Remove Exported Recovery Copies removes only the matching local archives included in that export. It does not remove the current notes, previous snapshot or exported file.

The previous snapshot is replaced by later normal saves. Recovery storage has limits; reaching a limit blocks the operation rather than silently discarding old archives. This recovery system is not a secure-erasure feature or a complete version history.

Deleting information

Deleting a note removes it from the active collection and removes its associated daily times. Prior contents can remain in the previous snapshot, recovery archives, exports, device backups or system-rendered widget snapshots. Deleting a note does not guarantee immediate removal from every copy or system interface.

Deleting the app can remove its local notes and recovery copies. It does not delete files you exported through Files, copies shared with other people or services, or backups managed by your device or file provider. Manage those copies separately with the relevant service. Save a normal backup outside the app before deleting Note Porch if you want to keep your notes.

Note Porch does not promise forensic secure erasure. Support cannot delete or restore information held only on your device or in a provider account it cannot access.

Device backups and system services

Your device or computer backup settings may include Note Porch's local data. Apple and file providers may process diagnostics, connection information or other system data under their own settings and privacy practices. These services are separate from Note Porch's local note storage and are not an app-operated backend or analytics service.

If you contact support

If you email daniele@duck.com, the recipient receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Note Porch does not automatically send note contents or diagnostics to support. Include only information needed to explain the issue, and remove private information from screenshots or examples.

Support correspondence is used to answer your request and investigate the issue you report. Email providers handle delivery and storage under their own privacy practices. For questions about information you have sent to support, or to request its correction or deletion, contact the same address. Such a request does not give support access to notes stored only on your device.

These support pages

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Their content includes no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or third-party fonts. GitHub may process connection information, such as an IP address, when delivering the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices. Visiting this website does not give us access to your notes.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its practices change. The date above identifies this version.

For privacy questions, contact daniele@duck.com. For help with notes, widgets or backups, visit Note Porch support.
