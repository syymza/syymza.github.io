Fuel, service and receipts.

Keep your vehicle records together.

Motoring Shelf is a manual vehicle logbook for iPhone and iPad. Record fuel stops, servicing and other expenses, keep receipt photos, and export your history when you need it.

Start with your garage

  1. Add a vehicle and choose its distance, fuel-volume and currency settings.
  2. Add a fuel, service or expense record. For a fuel record, enter the date, cost, odometer reading and a fuel volume greater than zero. For service and other expenses, add the date and cost, then any odometer reading or notes you want to keep.
  3. Review that vehicle’s history and recorded spending. The app uses the information you enter; it does not track trips or read an odometer automatically.

Fuel calculations

Mark whether a fill is full, partial or follows a missed fill-up. Fuel-economy intervals depend on these choices and entered odometer readings. An incomplete interval may not show an economy result. Check entered figures before relying on a summary.

Service targets and notifications

Set a target date or odometer reading for a service. Mileage status uses your latest entered odometer; it does not track your driving. Optional local notifications require permission. The app schedules up to the next 60 eligible dated targets at 9 AM local time. Open the app after travel to refresh times. iOS settings and Focus can delay or silence delivery.

Follow the manufacturer’s maintenance schedule and professional advice. This app does not inspect or diagnose a vehicle or guarantee a complete service history.

Receipt photos

Use the system Photos picker to choose up to eight photos per record. A cloud-only photo may need to download first. The app stores resized JPEG copies, not the original files. New photo imports do not copy original EXIF, GPS or camera metadata, but information visible in the photo remains visible.

Images are stored at up to 2,200 pixels on their longest edge. Each selected file must fit within the 50 MB input limit; stored copies are limited to 8 MB each, with 70 MB of receipt-image data per ledger. Keep originals separately if you need full-resolution records.

Choose the right export

  • CSV: the chosen vehicle’s fuel, service and expense records, including notes and receipt counts. Image bytes are not included. CSV is not a restorable backup.
  • Service-history PDF: vehicle details and service records, with their notes and attached receipt images.
  • Full backup: all vehicles, records, service targets and receipt images. Use this to move or restore your data.

Review files before sharing them. Notes and receipt images may contain personal information. The app adds no encryption to exported backups; choose a trusted storage or sharing destination.

Restore, Undo and recovery

Restoring a backup replaces all current records. It does not merge the two histories. A local recovery copy is kept first. Make your own full backup before restoring or changing phones.

Undo reverses only the most recent change in the current app session. A later edit, app termination or backup restore clears it. In Settings, Manage recovery files lets you export or delete an individually selected previous file. Recovery files are separate from normal backups and may contain damaged or old data; recovery is not guaranteed.

If saving or importing fails, keep the app installed, check device storage and keep the original file. Deleting the app removes its local container. Copies in Files, shared destinations and Apple device backups are managed separately.

Local records and language

Your saved records work offline without an account. There is no automatic cross-device sync, advertising or analytics. In Settings, Change app language opens the app’s iOS language settings. If Language is not shown, first add another language in iOS Settings → General → Language & Region.
