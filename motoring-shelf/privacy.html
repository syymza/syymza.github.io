Privacy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Provider and contact

Motoring Shelf is provided by Daniele Zanni. For privacy or support questions, contact daniele@duck.com.

Records stored on your device

The app stores vehicle names and details, fuel/service/expense records, costs, notes, service targets, preferences and selected receipt images locally. It does not require an account or send your records to a developer-operated server. There is no advertising, analytics, location tracking, automatic mileage collection or app-managed cloud-sync service.

Apple device backups may include app data according to your device and iCloud settings. Device backup services are separate from this app.

Receipt photos you select

The system Photos picker shares only the items you select rather than granting access to your whole library. A cloud-only item may need its provider to download it before import. New photo imports are resized and stored as JPEG copies without copying the source EXIF, GPS or camera metadata. Personal information visible in the picture is not removed. The app does not preserve or alter the original photo file.

Backups and files you share

A full backup contains vehicles, records, service targets and stored receipt-image data. CSV exports contain records, notes and receipt counts, without image bytes. Service-history PDFs contain vehicle details, service records, service notes and attached receipt images. These files can contain personal information.

You choose where to save or share exported files through Apple’s share sheet. The developer does not automatically receive them. The app adds no encryption to exported backups. Your chosen provider may store or sync them under its own settings and terms. Review each destination and file before sharing.

Optional local notifications

Service notifications require iOS permission. Vehicle and service names can appear in notification previews, controlled by iOS settings. No remote notification service is used. When notifications are disabled, the app asks iOS to remove its pending and delivered service notifications. With notifications enabled and reliable records available, it also removes delivered previews for deleted, completed or no-longer-dated targets. Incomplete targets can retain an earlier preview, including earlier wording. iOS controls actual delivery and removal timing.

Deletion, Undo and recovery copies

Edits and deletions are saved immediately. Only the most recent change can be undone in the current app session; a later edit, app termination or backup restore clears Undo. Restoring a backup replaces current data after confirmation and keeps a previous local file first. Settings can export or permanently delete an individually selected recovery file after confirmation. Recovery files may contain older or damaged data, remain until removed or the app container is deleted, and are not included in normal full backups. Recovery is not guaranteed.

Deleting records does not remove copies previously exported or shared elsewhere. Deleting the app removes its local container subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior. Manage outside copies through Files, the receiving provider or Apple’s backup settings.

Temporary export copies

The app attempts to remove its temporary file after sharing finishes. Interrupted sharing or cleanup failure may leave a copy. When the app runs, it also performs limited cleanup of inactive app-owned exports older than 24 hours, while protecting active shares and excluding recovery files. This is not a guaranteed deletion deadline: file protection, the device clock and iOS behavior can defer cleanup.

Support correspondence

If you email support, your address and the information you provide are handled by the email providers involved and used to respond to your request. Only include information you choose to share. The support link supplies an app-name subject and does not attach your records, pictures, backups or diagnostics automatically. Contact the address above about support correspondence or deletion requests.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, third-party fonts or cookies set by this site.

Changes

This policy will be reviewed when app features or data flows change. The effective date above identifies the published version.
